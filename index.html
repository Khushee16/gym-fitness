Free tool

Know your starting point.

Body Mass Index is a quick, rough guide to where your weight sits for your height. It takes ten seconds — and it helps our coaches point you to the right programme when you join.

BMI is only a starting point: it can't tell muscle from fat, so a high BMI in a very muscular person doesn't mean the same thing. Treat it as a guide, not a verdict.
